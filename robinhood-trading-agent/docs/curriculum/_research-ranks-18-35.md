# FT Global MBA 2025 ranks 18–35 — public investment / AM / HF-related courses

Filter applied: investment management, asset management, portfolio management, securities analysis, hedge funds, alternative investments, risk management (investment), equity research.  
Public pages only. Course offerings change yearly; titles below are as listed on school/catalog pages at research time.

## 19 — UCLA Anderson

**Relevant courses:** Investment Management / Security Analysis & Investment Management (MGMT 232A); Fixed-Income Markets (232B); Market and Credit Risk Management (232E); Hedge Funds (MGMT 298D); Anderson Student Asset Management (MGMT 457); Behavioral Finance (232F)  

**Public URLs:**  
- https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/faculty-and-research/finance/finance-course-recommendations

- https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/degrees/full-time-mba/academics/capstone-project/anderson-student-asset-management-asam  

**Public syllabus / detailed description?** **Yes** (course blurbs + Kayne Investment Management specialization requirements).  
- Digest: 232A = portfolio construction, active/passive choice, alpha measurement (not stock-picking). ASAM = student teams back-test and run strategies with ~$1M real capital. Kayne specialization requires 430 + 232A plus two electives from a published list including Hedge Funds.
